# Growth notes

Weekly learnings from post performance (written by the Monday morning run).

## Week of 2026-11-09 → 2026-11-15 (written 2026-11-16)

- Best: app posts with a real photo behind glass. Solace Stays travel app had the top reach (47) and 6 comments; Lumen language app 28; Penny AI finance chat app had the most saves (2). Dashboards and websites sat at 10–27 reach, and saves/shares are close to zero across the board (4 saves, 0 shares in 15 posts).
- Watch: likes fell from ~95–178 on the first week's posts to 3–17 on posts from 13 Nov on, while reach held at 20–47, so the early like counts were not organic reach; judge posts by reach, saves and comments, not likes. Ledgerly (10 reach) was the weakest.
- Do more: photo-led app screens and "the AI answers a real question" hooks; give dashboards and websites one saveable idea per post (a component breakdown or a clear before/after) and make the save prompt name what to save it for.
