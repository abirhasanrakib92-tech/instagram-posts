Philosophy

We believe a good cup shouldn't need a manual. Kettle & Crow roasts coffee the week you order it, from farms we visit every harvest, so the flavour you taste is the place it came from, not the shelf it sat on.

Roasted on Tuesdays, rested four days, shipped Thursdays. Every bag carries its roast date and the name of the farm.
