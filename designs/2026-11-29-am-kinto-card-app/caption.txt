Three cards, four currencies, and you still don't know what you can spend.
Fintech wallet app UI design in dark mode: card stack, multi-currency balance and weekly spending cap

Kinto folds every card and pocket into one balance, then puts a weekly cap right under it with one lit bar for your biggest day. Everything sits on a 4pt grid, so the screen stays calm even with this much money on it.
